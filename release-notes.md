## XTA

A read-only fork of [QuaX](https://github.com/Teskann/QuaX). Same idea — read X
without an account of X's choosing, keep what you follow on your own device —
with the plugins, feeds and fixes below on top.

### What's new

**Threads, read on your own terms.** A new plugin, and nothing in it talks to
Meta. Posts come from an RSSHub instance you run; profiles come from your own
Xy server, which answers the guest lookups Threads will not serve directly.
Add accounts by handle — `zuck`, `@zuck` or a pasted profile link all work —
look one up to see the avatar, bio, verified badge and counts before you
follow, and the accounts sit in your subscription groups like every other
source. Neither half is required: without the Xy server you still get posts,
without RSSHub you can still look profiles up. There is no shared default
instance on purpose, and no credential is compiled into the app.

**Picture-in-picture.** A video can shrink into a floating window and keep
playing while you use the rest of the phone — the one thing fullscreen could
never do. There is also a fill/fit control now, for when a clip leaves black
bands down the sides of your screen.

Everything the player already had — speed, quality, subtitles, download — is
where it was.
